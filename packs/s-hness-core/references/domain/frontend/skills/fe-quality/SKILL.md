# Fe Quality

Select quality requirements from the approved specification, user environment, supported browsers, device constraints, content, and measured bottlenecks. Avoid generic optimization or accessibility claims without observable evidence.

Read [frontend quality profile](../../references/frontend-quality-profile.md).

## Output

Define semantic and keyboard requirements, focus and announcement behavior, zoom and contrast expectations, breakpoint and container behavior, localization growth, loading and failure resilience, asset and rendering budgets, measurement method, and acceptance thresholds.

## Completion

Quality design is complete when important requirements are measurable and map to verification. Do not claim conformance or performance improvement without tests or measurements.
